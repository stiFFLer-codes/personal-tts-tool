• Context: educational / training. A DISCUSSION between {{SPEAKERS}}, e.g. two university students
  planning or reviewing an assignment, presentation, field trip or research project, sometimes with
  their tutor giving feedback.
• This part tests understanding of OPINIONS, ATTITUDES, AGREEMENT and DISAGREEMENT, not just facts.
  Speakers suggest, doubt, change their minds, agree only partly ("I see your point, but…"), and the
  answer often depends on what they finally decide or on who holds which view.
• Academic but conversational vocabulary (methodology, data, sources, references, deadline, survey,
  sample, findings, literature review...). Speakers refer to their course, lecturer and reading.
• Length: {{WORDS}} words of discussion (about 4–6 minutes), plus narrator lines.
• Typical traps: one speaker suggests an option and the other rejects it; both mention a problem but
