Script format cheat-sheet
  • Name: text, a speaker line. Lines with no label are read by the Narrator.
  • [Pause: you now have 30 seconds to look at questions 7 to 10.]: narrator reads it, then real 30 s silence. [Pause 5] = silence only.
  • W-H-I-T-F-I-E-L-D is spelled out letter by letter; phone numbers are read digit by digit.
  • Title: … and Voices: Sam=bm_lewis, Clara=bf_emma are optional header lines.
  • === QUESTIONS ===: shown during the exam. Gaps like 1 ________ become answer boxes.
  • === ANSWERS ===: 2. 23(rd) | twenty-third. | = alternatives, ( ) = optional, 21-22. B, D = any order.
  • Need scripts? Use the prompt in docs/CLAUDE_SCRIPT_PROMPT.md with Claude.
